Privacy Policy — Globorama

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Overview

Globorama is a fully offline geography guessing game. We respect your privacy and have designed the app to operate without collecting, storing, or transmitting any personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data.

Globorama does not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your location
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Send data to any server
  • Use cookies or similar technologies
  • Contain advertising
  • Include any third-party SDKs that collect data

Data Storage

All game data (statistics, game history, and preferences) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

In-App Purchases

Globorama offers an optional one-time in-app purchase for unlimited play. All purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to any payment information. Your purchase history is managed by your Apple ID account.

Third-Party Services

Globorama does not integrate with third-party analytics or advertising services. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store — see Apple's privacy policy for details. The app contains optional links to Google Maps and Norli.no that open in Safari — these external sites have their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Globorama does not collect any data from any users, including children. The app is suitable for all ages (rated 4+).

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the updated version will be included in the app update and posted at the support URL.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the app's support page on GitHub.
